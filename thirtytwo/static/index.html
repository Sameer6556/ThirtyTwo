How it works

  1. A tooth detector finds and numbers every tooth.
  2. A classifier checks each tooth up close.
  3. Detectors scan the whole X-ray.
  4. Nine models vote, and boxes that several models agree on score higher.

Some of the models were trained further on 1,961 extra X-rays that the system labelled itself.

Accuracy

On the 50 official DENTEX validation X-rays, the full system scores 48.8 AP and Quick mode 48.1, against 38.0 for a standard YOLO11m detector. Over five random splits of the DENTEX data, the full system averages 47.8 AP.

AP is a strict score from 0 to 100 for how closely the boxes overlap the experts' boxes, not the percentage of correct answers.

Limitations

  • Trained only on DENTEX, which comes from three hospitals in one country.
  • Not tested on other X-ray machines or in other clinics.
  • It can miss findings and raise false alarms.
  • Periapical lesions are the hardest class.
