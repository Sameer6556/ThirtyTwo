Thirty Two

Accuracy

On the 50 official DENTEX validation X-rays, the full system scores 48.8 AP and Quick mode 48.1, against 38.0 for a standard YOLO11m detector. Over five random splits of the DENTEX data, the full system averages 47.8 AP.

Limitations

  • Trained only on DENTEX, which comes from three hospitals in one country.
  • Not tested on other X-ray machines or in other clinics.
  • It can miss findings and raise false alarms.
  • Periapical lesions are the hardest class.

How it was measured

The score is the DENTEX challenge's average precision (COCO AP), averaged over box overlaps from 50% to 95%. Every setting was chosen on 70 other X-rays, so the official 50 never chose anything. This app reproduces the numbers on an ordinary CPU.
